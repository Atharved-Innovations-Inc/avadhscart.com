About Avadhscart

Building the future of retail from Jabalpur, India

What the Avadh Dash Cart does

A fully autonomous checkout system embedded inside a standard-sized shopping cart.

⚙

Robotic Pick & Place

3-axis Cartesian gantry with soft-grip Fin-Ray fingers picks, sorts, and places every item into the right bin automatically.

⚖

Real-Time Verification

Dual camera system + barcode scanner + load cell. Every item is identified, weighed, and cross-checked against the product catalog.

📷

Dual Camera Vision

Overhead gantry camera for precise pick coordinates. Intake camera for item identification and barcode reading.

⚡

Battery Powered

12V LiFePO4 battery provides 8+ hours of operation. Plug-in charging between shifts. No trailing cables.

🔨

Enclosed & Silent

Fully opaque enclosure — shoppers never see the gantry. Under 40 dB at 1m, quieter than a refrigerator.

💳

Digital Checkout

Running total on a built-in tablet display. Tap to pay via UPI, card, or wallet. Walk out. Done.

How It Works

Four steps. Zero scanning.

Drop your item in and keep shopping. The cart handles everything else.

01

Drop In

Place any item into the intake area at the top. A conveyor draws it to the scanning station.

02

Scan + Weigh

Camera and barcode scanner identify the item. Load cell verifies weight against catalog. Mismatches flagged instantly.

03

Pick + Sort

Robotic gantry picks and places the item into the correct bin — fragile items separated, heavy items at the bottom.

04

Pay + Go

Total updates live on the display. Pay via UPI, tap, or card. Walk out — no queue, no billing counter.
